Life is short. You probably already know that. But how should we spend our limited time on this planet? What pursuits should we undertake? Who do we strive to become?

I've always been in a constant state of existential panic, as someone who, from a very young age, spent countless hours pondering the meaning and purpose of existence. Nothing my parents and culture could every teach me could satisfy the salient questions on my mind. 

I write this because for the very first time in my life, I don't really have a clue on what do next. Recently starting a 9-5, a weird sense of finality is brewing within me. Is this it? Is working for the rest of my life really my destiny?

 
I plan on reading [Tuesdays with Morrie](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/6900.Tuesdays_with_Morrie) this week. Hopefully I end up finding the answer?
