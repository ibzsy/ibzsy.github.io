## Goodbye 2024: 2025 is upon us!  
I think I speak for everyone when I say that these past couple of years have passed by in instant.
I'm not sure if it was the collective amnesia or time dilation we felt as a result of the 2020 COVID pandemic, 
or if it’s just because we're getting older, but time is passing by quickly. I've always struggled with coming to terms with the 
[concept of time](/on-life/), and how best we can utilize it in-order to live a fulfilled life. 
And as the dawn of the new year approaches, I think it'll be worthwhile to set some resolutions I can work to achieve in the coming year. 

## New Year's Resolutions: They're Flawed.  
Like millions around the world, I've tried setting resolutions year after year, only to never achieve any or simply forget about them. And as you know, long-term concepts are difficult for us to comprehend and act upon. It's much easier for us to plan and act upon achieving a goal within the timescale of a week, rather then a year. Therefore, I’m going to **screw** resolutions in favor of quarterly goals. These time periods are much shorter and easier to comprehend, and there must be some wisdom as to why economies around the world run on this timescale.

## Q1 2025: Building Foundations  
I'll be turning 23 in Q1 2025, and while I still struggle to figure out how the f*@k I’m already 23, I’m aware of the fact that, in reality, I lack a solid foundation for personal growth. I don't want the rest of this post to become a self-hate novel, but even though I’m not an utter bum, I can always improve.

Like many "Gen-Z'ers" (I hate that word), I struggle with utilizing my free time in a productive manner, 
spending much of my time scrolling YouTube Shorts, Reddit, and Twitter searching for that dopamine hit. 
I average around 3 hours a day on my phone, which, while it may not seem like a lot, adds up to approximately 
an entire 24-hour day per week spent on a fruitless activity. That’s something I want to put an end to.

Another thing I want to do is become much wiser as an individual. While this is definitely something that's going to be a lifelong project, it's something that, at present, I'm not actively pursuing. While I'm definitely intellectually competent at my job and in other areas of my life, I feel as if the depth of my knowledge is quite shallow, and lacking personal contributions. I want to not only work on deepening my knowledge base, but also work on its breadth.

And of-course, all these goals are underpinned by a strong foundation of discipline, and thats something I want to work upon as well. Without working towards my goals via consistency driven by discipline, I might as well give up now.

**_And… you know what? I think that's enough goals._ But how do I make these 'goals' attainable?**

### Talk is Cheap: Creating Attainable Q1 Goals  
Talk is cheap. I can talk all day long about how much I want a six-pack and the many recipes I’m going to try to attain it, 
but as long as it remains talk, I'll forever be doomed to have a beer belly (I don’t actually have one, just for comedic effect).

#### Goal 1: Hit the Gym 3-4 Times a Week  
Probably the most important of the three. 
This should give me the confidence to propel myself toward my other goals. It helps with self-esteem and belief, and, of course, it’s healthy.

#### Goal 2: Digital Detox  
The attainable goals here are a bit harder to define, but there’s one site I know is a constant time sucker and wasteful: Reddit. No website hits the same dopamine spots for me like Reddit; the variety is unbelievable. 
I want to completely cut it out of my life. It’s brought nothing but stress, anxiety, and depression.
I want to reduce the amount of time I spend mindlessly browsing the internet. 

I should strive it hit 2 hours maximum on my phone. 

#### Goal 3: Read 10 Books  
Pretty simple and self-explanatory. 
I’ve always wanted to become what you'd call a "reader." 
I’m more of a non-fiction person, so books on history and personal development are the genres I want to explore for the next quarter.

#### Goal 4: Pick Up A Social Activity
Ever since graduating from University, it's been really hard making friends with common interests and hobbies. I really want to improve this situation, and I think joining a social club centered around a hobby I have would be perfect. 
 
## Closing Remarks 

> الوقت كالسيف إن لم تقطعه قطعك 
>
> Time is like a sword, if you don't cut it, it will cut you - Arabic Proverb

I remember coming across this proverb in my middle school Arabic class, and at the time, I couldn’t quite grasp its meaning. 
How could something abstract become something physical, like a sword?
But now, I understand. Time is a powerful yet dangerous tool we’ve created. We can either wield it, or become its victims.

I'd rather do the former! 😀
